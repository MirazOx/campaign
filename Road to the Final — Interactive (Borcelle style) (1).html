Pre-empting the peer critique

We name our own platform break

The weakest link, flagged first.

The likely break

The jump from free social content to paid streaming. A casual fan hits the paywall and drops off.

Our answer

The free data sheet, prediction game and ticket lottery keep non-payers inside the ecosystem — the paywall is never the only path forward.

Second likely flag — influencer content feeling like lifestyle, not journalism. Our answer: influencers own atmosphere and access, then hand off to the expert shows for substance. A clear division of labour protects credibility while borrowing reach.
